## Full text

> The fastest way to waste money on Meta is running the right ad at the wrong awareness level.
> 
> Here's the map, and what happens when you get it wrong.
> 
> Unaware: founder ad, podcast clip, yapper ad, AI animation, voiceless text overlay.
> Get it wrong and you're pitching a product to someone who hasn't admitted they have a problem.
> 
> Problem-aware: UGC, native statics, 3 reasons why.
> Get it wrong and you're explaining a mechanism to someone still deciding if this is even a thing.
> 
> Solution-aware: long-form VSL, text wall ad.
> Get it wrong and you're showing reviews to someone who doesn't understand what they'd be reviewing.
> 
> Product-aware: raw honest review, testimonial mashup, comment reply ad, review static.
> Get it wrong and you're discounting to someone who was never sold in the first place.
> 
> Most aware: urgency offer static, product static.
> Get it wrong and you're telling a story to someone with their card already out.
> 
> Same product. Five different ads.
> 
> Anyone running one ad across all five is paying for four of them to fail.
