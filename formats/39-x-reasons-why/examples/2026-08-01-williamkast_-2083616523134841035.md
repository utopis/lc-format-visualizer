## Full text

> We've scaled accounts to $30k/day on 3-4 core angles.
> 
> The secret is not in running lots of angles, but in lots of ad formats.
> 
> These are 15 ad formats to repackage your winning ads into:
> 
> 1. Founders Ads
> 
> One of the best ad formats to run.
> 
> Very authentic and impossible to copy.
> 
> The founder will always convey most conviction.
> 
> 2. Yappers Ads
> 
> Wins against UGC ads most time.
> 
> The raw non cut yapping is great for trust.
> 
> 3. AI Animation Ads
> 
> Works execellently because it looks like entertainment but sells.
> 
> This is slowly taking UGC over.
> 
> 4. UGC Ads
> 
> People know by now it's paid but this is still king.
> 
> If you show visual proof of what you claim, people don't care if it's staged or not.
> 
> 5. Voiceless Textoverlay Ads
> 
> A variation of UGC ads.
> 
> Show the key points, and highlight them with short phrases.
> 
> 6. Longform VSL Ads
> 
> Great to take the work off an advertorial or longer funnel that would be usually needed.
> 
> This can get someone from unaware to buying-ready/
> 
> 7. Lifestyle Static Ads
> 
> Great to show your product or what it can do authentically.
> 
> 8. Carousel Ads
> 
> Just like a video but in static format.
> 
> The first slide is the hook, the other slides the body, the last one the CTA.
> 
> 9. TikTok/IG Reply Ad
> 
> Great to cover common questions and objections.
> 
> Also great to repackage the core message into.
> 
> 10. Text Wall Ad
> 
> Great to convey a small message in an engaging format.
> 
> Similar to a static ad but you have more copy than just a headline.
> 
> 11. Product Focused Static Ad
> 
> Great to highlight the product and whatever it does better than other products.
> 
> Especially strong for product aware audiences.
> 
> 12. Static Review Ad
> 
> Builds social proof.
> 
> You can package your message into the review.
> 
> 13. Camouflage Ad
> 
> This is the most organic way to run a static ad.
> 
> The static ad is just like an organic photo post, no copy whatsoever.
> 
> The conversion mechanism is in the long ad text.
> 
> 14. Branded Static Ad
> 
> Great to build branding - rather a supporter in the ad account.
> 
> But good as a way of repurposing winning messages.
> 
> 15. Branded Product Video
> 
> Same as 14.
> 
> Only makes sense for a BOF audience or big brands having lots of awareness.
> 
> Great for branding.
> 
> Take your best ads and repackage them into all of the above.
