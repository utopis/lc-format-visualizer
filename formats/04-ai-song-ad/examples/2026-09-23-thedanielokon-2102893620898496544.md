> HOW TO CREATE BANGER AI SONG ADS:
> 
> One of the biggest things to remember is you are trying to do TWO things really well. 
> 
> 1. Storytelling: This is the heart of the winning Suno song. You need to try to bring people through a story arc. This is why AI drama videos are doing so well.
> 
> You need to show some problem that is clearly an issue, or something that could be quite outlandish. You can see Resilia does this well with their songs. 
> 
> It's the EXTREME problem. Your wife isn't distant, she is OPENLY cheating on you. Why? Because you're overweight.
> 
> So there's drama here. You literally are following this guy's story and now you are invested in what happens to him. 
> 
> The payoff doesn't usually happen until a minute or two into the song, sometimes even later. 
> 
> 2. Direct Response: This is where things get a little more difficult. You need to be able to tie in selling the product.
> 
> Let's be honest, most people are terrible at making conversion-focused creative. You need to be able to hit on the main benefits of the product, reviews, offer, cta, etc toward the end. 
> 
> The arc of any good suno ad is: dramatic storyline or curiosity hook -> catchy tune that walks someone through the problem -> a hero that brings the solution to them -> the payoff/transformation -> selling the product hard.
> 
> If you do any of these steps badly, you have something that won't work. You also need to have a few things right:
> 
> 1. Customer Avatar matching the music genre
> 2. Major desire that is clear in the beginning
> 3. Genuinely good music and fun lyrics
> 
> Whenever we have banger song ads, people LOVE them. The comments are insane.
