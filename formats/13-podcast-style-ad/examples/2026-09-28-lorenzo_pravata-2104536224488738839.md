## Full text

> Just run ads that don't look like ads
> 
> I don't understand why you would ever build your whole Meta account around anything else
> 
> UGC:
> 
> - Every brand in your niche is running the same creator holding the same product
> - People scroll past it because they know exactly what it is in one second
> - Creators cost more every quarter and half the videos come back unusable
> - Andromeda reads 50 near-identical UGC videos as one ad
> 
> Over-polished ads:
> 
> - They look like an ad in the first frame, so the viewer is gone before the hook
> - Weeks of production for a single concept
> - Too expensive to test at the volume Meta wants now
> - Hard to justify the budget when you don't know which angle wins yet
> 
> Discount statics:
> 
> - They train your customers to wait for the next sale
> - Your margin pays for every single test
> - They work on warm audiences, then stall when you try to scale cold
> - Any competitor can copy the offer tomorrow
> 
> Influencer whitelisting:
> 
> - You're paying for someone else's audience, and the ad is an afterthought
> - One creator, one voice, one angle
> - Hard to iterate once the video is live
> - Your results depend on someone else's schedule
> 
> Stealth ads:
> 
> - Podcast clips, street interviews and skits look like content, so people actually watch
> - The first 3 seconds feel organic, so your hook gets a real chance
> - One winning angle can run in 5 formats, and Meta reads each one as new
> - You can shoot volume without casting a new creator for every concept
> - The format carries the authority, so the product can come in late
> - Every winner gives you the script for the next format
> - Competitors can't copy a format they don't know how to produce
> - Fatigue slows down because the account never looks the same twice
> - Studio actors mean you own the footage for every future iteration
> - The account compounds every month
> 
> On one pet brand we changed ~95% of the creatives and kept the same campaign structure
> 
> Spend went from $29K to $150K a month in 60 days, and cost per result dropped from $188 to $124
> 
> Just run ads that don't look like ads
>
