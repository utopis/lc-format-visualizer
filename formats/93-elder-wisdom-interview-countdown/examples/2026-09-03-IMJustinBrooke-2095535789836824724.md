> Passing on my best wisdom, pt 8
> 
> 21yrs of Hook Writing Advice in <600 Words
> 
> As I thought about everything I could probably teach you, one thing stood out as the most important lesson. That lesson is "When you increase relevance, you increase conversion."
> 
> This is why everyone gets fanatical about ad targeting, keywords, headlines, etc. All of that works BECAUSE it increases relevance.
> 
> Lets do an example:
> 
> Lose weight.
> 
> For the majority of the population those two words alone can attract attention. Now, lets increase the relevance.
> 
> Lose 30lbs.
> 
> Instantly more attractive.
> 
> And we can do it again.
> 
> Lose 30lbs this summer.
> 
> The more we know about our target customer, the more relevant we can make our ad and the more relevant the ad is, the greater it will lead to conversions.
> 
> For example, if we are targeting younger women we might change it to...
> 
> Drop 30lbs Fast & Get Your Dream "Hot Girl" Body This Summer
> 
> But for any older woman, we might have to say...
> 
> Melt Off Stubborn Fat Tissue & Tighten Up "Flappy" Arms
> 
> The guys at the highest levels are using IP location detection to take this even further by adding specific location data into their headlines. They can dynamically insert local imagery into the images and backgrounds, and city or state names into the copy.
> 
> Tennessee Woman Discovers Superfood That Melts Stubborn "Flappy" Arm Fat
> 
> One more point on relevance...
> 
> The most hated advice I have ever given is to create 1 ad and 1 landing page for each keyword or interest that you target. Yes, it's a lot more work, but... That relevance goes WAY UP and so does your conversion rate.
> 
> Now, lets breakdown what I did in that Tennessee woman headline.
> 
> Tennessee = Location
> 
> Woman = Gender
> 
> Discovers Superfood = Mechanism
> 
> Melts Stubborn = Objection killer
> 
> "Flappy" Arm Fat = Deep rooted pain
> 
> It's a template. A system you can rinse and reuse for any market to produce highly compelling ad copy over and over again.
> 
> Miami Man Reveals 7 Words That Make Cold Approach Easy & Rejection Rate Drops
> 
> Miami = Location
> 
> Man = Gender
> 
> Reveals 7 Words = Mechanism
> 
> Make Cold Approach Easy = Objection killer
> 
> Rejection Rate = Deep rooted pain
> 
> You can move them around too, they don't need to be in that order.
> 
> Men Using Cold Approaches on Miami Beach Get Rejected Far Less Thanks to These 7 Words
> 
> But what if your market isn't attract to "away from pain" angles?
> 
> Every market is either trying to get away from pain, or towards pleasure. So you need to test both angles, until you know which trigger works in your market.
> 
> For example, YOU... the money twitter or internet marketer audience is a towards pleasure market. Y'all would much rather learn how to make $100/day, than how to save $100/day.
> 
> ❌ 7 Free Things Tennessee Residents Can Do to Save Over $1,000/ Month
> 
> ✅ These 3 Tennessee Business Models Are Making Thousands for New Startups
