# Compliance baseline for every format (applies to all docs/formats/*)

Read this before producing anything from a format file. Format files only add format-specific notes on top of this.

1. **Product claims (LC):** say **14K PVD** gold (never "18k", "solid gold", "fused 18k"). "Waterproof / shower-proof / won't tarnish" only as worded on the current PDP and backed by LC's own testing. Customer counts: use the verified figure from the brand brain (300k+), not "450K+", until reconciled. No fake scarcity ("only 3 left") unless inventory is true; countdowns only for real sale end dates.
2. **AI disclosure:** any realistic AI person, voice or scene → turn on TikTok "AI-generated" label, Meta "AI info" / made-with-AI disclosure where prompted, YouTube altered/synthetic disclosure. Never present an AI avatar as a real customer giving a testimonial. AI characters may *dramatize* a scenario; reviews/testimonials must come from real customers (quote real reviews verbatim with permission).
3. **Endorsements:** creators/affiliates use #ad / Paid Partnership / TikTok "Promotional content" toggle. Niche/fan pages run by LC must say they are LC-run in bio ("by Louise Carter") — no undisclosed "unbranded" pages pretending to be independent.
4. **No bulk/spoofed accounts:** no device/phone farms, proxies to fake locations, purchased "warmed" accounts, or dozens of creator-style accounts operated by LC. Max a handful of disclosed LC-owned accounts, each a real, distinct editorial concept, operated from normal devices. (Several sources in this feed describe proxy/phone-farm setups; we copy only the creative method.)
5. **Don't copy others' assets:** borrow *structure* (hook type, pacing, slide order), never footage, photos, characters, voices, music, or exact scripts from competitors or creators. Licensed/original music only on ads (song-ad files: LC-owned AI songs with commercial rights on the plan used). No branded/IP characters (Pixar/LEGO/Disney look-alikes are a style reference only; no logos, no named characters).
6. **Authority figures:** no fake doctors/dermatologists/pharmacists. Use real, credentialed people with disclosure, or clearly-fictional characters with no credential claims. Jewelry has no medical claims — avoid "hypoallergenic" unless documented for the alloy and finish.
7. **Shock hooks:** drama/relationship hooks are OK; no sexual content involving minors or step-family, no implying real people did things, no harassment. LC tone = warm, witty, confident — not fear-bait.
8. **Platform rules:** no engagement-bait ("comment X to get") on Meta ads; follow Meta/TikTok policy on before/after (jewelry before/after = styling, not body change).
9. **Measurement:** every asset carries a campaign/asset ID in UTM (`utm_content=<format>-<NN>-<variant>`) so Omni can attribute revenue and new customers.
