## Full text

> Sweepstakes is one of our fastest growing campaigns at Ridge.
> 
> With Tony Hawk onboard and over a half-million in prizes, we drove ~50% revenue growth YoY at a better MER — making it our first multi-8-figure sweeps campaign.
> 
> The budget breakdown is below — and a few thoughts on what drove the channel shifts:
> 
> 1/ The celebrity endorsement unlocked some new scale for us, with TV most notably gaining budget share.
> 
> We scaled TV +71% YoY (exclusively linear) at roughly half last year's in-platform CPA — largely from significantly stronger response rates.
> 
> Attribution is difficult with TV, but we validated performance with seeing "TV / Streaming" responses increased proportionately as a % of total responses.
> 
> 2/ Meta gained share of budget (40% → 47%) as we spent 35% more YoY at a 14% better ROAS.
> 
> I predicted earlier this year that Meta would gain share of budget for brands as advertisers embraced creative volume, andromeda, new attribution models, etc.
> 
> We didn't see it in Q1 — and we'll see whether it continues through EOY — but interesting to see it take shape in this campaign.
> 
> 3/ Improved Meta performance largely came down to creative diversity: partnership + affiliate ads.
> 
> Not shown in the budget breakdown, but Sweepstakes 2025 saw ~3% of our Meta spend run through partner pages and 0% come from affiliate content.
> 
> This year it was nearly 40% combined — creator and partner ads converting at a higher ROAS than our baseline account.
> 
> All in, we launched over 2,200 ads during the campaign, +50% YoY.
> 
> 4/ TikTok had the largest YoY % of budget increase (4.1% → 9.1%) as we basically doubled our investment in GMV Max.
> 
> ROAS is still GMV-inflated so I'd take the improvement with a grain of salt, but this is the trajectory I was hoping for when I said TikTok was one of our biggest opportunities at the end of last year.
> 
> Creators posted 5,269 videos on TikTok during the campaign — actually down from June–July, as we better aligned incentives around quality over raw volume.
> 
> To the previous point, we are shifting some of our affiliate efforts to be more Trybe-based as we more directly incentivize creators to generate high-performing Meta ads vs TikTok Shop sales/GMV.
> 
> 5/ The trims: Snapchat down ~75%, and X basically went to zero.
> 
> After a strong run on X last year (and a prediction we'd spend more this year), we're letting performance set the budget — and those dollars went to the channels that earned them.

>
