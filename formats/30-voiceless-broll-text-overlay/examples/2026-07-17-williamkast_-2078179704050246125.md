## Full text

> If you haven't tested these ad formats yet, you should:
> 
> 1. Founder talking head
> - Raw, low production, just the founder speaking about the product or problem
> - 30 seconds of genuine talk outperforms polished content.
> 
> 2. Voiceless B-roll ads
> - Mix of B-roll and product shots, no one talking
> - Script delivered through text overlay bullet points
> - Clean, fast, easy to scale.
> 
> 3. Yapper ads
> - Someone talking organically into the camera, completely uncut
> - No script feel, no editing
> - Builds trust faster than anything polished because it feels like a friend telling you about something
> 
> 4. AI educational videos
> - AI visuals explaining a concept or mechanism
> - Great for health brands where you need to educate before you sell.
> 
> 5. Camouflage ads
> - A native-looking image that blends into the feed like an organic post
> - Paired with long-form ad text using emotional storytelling from problem to solution to action
> - People don't realize it's an ad until they're invested.
> 
> The format isn't what makes these work though.
> 
> What makes them work is the message underneath and how well it speaks to the right persona at the right awareness stage.
> 
> The format gets attention. 
> 
> The message gets the conversion.
