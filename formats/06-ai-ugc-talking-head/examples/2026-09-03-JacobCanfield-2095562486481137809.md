## Full text

> The old model of UGC for ecom might is dead. 
> 
> My agency ran it and perfected it for over a year... but we have been pivoting to performance creative management. 
> 
> Old model: Pay $1,000 for 3-5 videos to a good creator. (maybe more for partnership access) and load them in the ad account and pray they work.
> 
> New model: Pay $0 for hundreds of videos and pay only off a comission when they work. Find winners weekly and move to scaling campaigns.
> 
> This is like TikTok shop.. but for Meta. (paid off % of ad spend or GMV)
> 
> We are currently managing multiple @TrybeUGC accounts for ecom brands. 
> 
> Here are some current stats for a few brands that  we onboarded and started managing starting August 1.
> 
> Brand 1: Onboarded 52 creators and 83 submitted videos for $12.7k meta sales generated.
> 
> Brand 2: Onboarded 163 creators with 258 submitted videos for $16.5k spend. (they were on pace for $40k in sales but reduced ad spend to redo their website mid August.)
> 
> Brand 3: Onboarded 70 creators with 43 video submissions. (creators are still getting samples) for $7,450 sales. 
> 
> I believe month 2 will be insane, so will check back in for october with more stats.
> 
> If you run an ecom brand and looking for performance creative management, hit the DM's.
