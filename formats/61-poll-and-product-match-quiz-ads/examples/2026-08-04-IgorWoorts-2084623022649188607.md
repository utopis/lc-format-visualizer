## Full text

> There is NO single landing page that works best for all your ads. It all depends on the awareness stage.
> 
> Unaware → listicle / quiz funnel (educate around the problem).
> 
> Problem-aware → advertorial / quiz funnel (explain WHY + personalize).
> 
> Solution-aware → quiz funnel / listicle / comparison page (sell the mechanism).
> 
> Product-aware → PDP / comparison page (proof + differentiation).
> 
> Most aware → offer page (give them a reason to buy NOW).
> 
> I still see too many brands sending unaware traffic straight to a PDP or collection page, while the visitor doesn't even understand the problem yet.
> 
> That's why your funnel needs to match the awareness stage. 
> 
> Different awareness = different LP types + goals. 
> 
> It's that simple.
