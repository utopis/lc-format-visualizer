## Full text

> Here's how you chadscale your way past $10k/day on cold traffic using quiz funnels.
> 
> Quiz funnels are the highest-converting destination for ecom brands right now.
> 
> And I still don't understand why most CPG and consumable brands are still sending cold traffic to a product page. 
> 
> I have seen quiz funnels converting cold traffic at 3-8%. 
> 
> Which is wild... but is also the #1 reason why you should be using them if you want your ad account to start printing more cash. 
> 
> So why does the quiz funnel work so well specifically for CPG and consumable brands?
> 
> Because personalization is the entire game.
> 
> Your customer is not buying another supplement. 
> 
> They are buying a solution to a specific problem they have been living with for months.
> 
> Their dog has been scratching since January and they have tried four different pet supplements.
> 
> They have been waking up exhausted for two years and melatonin stopped working.
> 
> A product page will always show them what you sell.
> 
> A quiz shows them that you understand what they are going through.
> 
> That shift from a brand talking about itself to a brand asking the right questions and reflecting the customer back at themselves is what changes conversion fundamentally.
> 
> Here is how I think about quiz funnel architecture for problem-solution products:
> 
> The first question is the hard segmenter. 
> 
> The one question whose answer routes 80% of the experience. 
> 
> For pet supplements: dog size or primary health concern. 
> 
> For sleep: whether the problem is falling asleep or staying asleep. 
> 
> For skincare: skin type or primary concern. 
> 
> The middle questions build those micro-commitments. 
> 
> If you are doing a good job here, by question four, they are psychologically invested in the outcome. They want to see the result.
> 
> The results page is a personalized recommendation that leads with their specific situation, names their specific problem, and presents the product offer as the answer (with social proof from someone in the exact same situation). 
> 
> Now here is the insight most people miss about quiz funnels.
> 
> Your best pre-sell landers are already 80% of the way to a quiz funnel.
> 
> If you have a pre-sell lander converting cold traffic, opening on the problem, agitating the pain, introducing the mechanism, building the proof, that's your quiz funnel structure. 
> 
> Same emotional journey, same psychological framework, but now the customer feels like the destination was built specifically for them.
> 
> Let's not forget about the fact that every quiz completion is first-party data.
> 
> You know their dog's age, size, and primary health concern.
> 
> You know their sleep problem type and how long they have had it.
> 
> You know their skin type and their biggest insecurity.
> 
> That data feeds your email flows, your segmentation, your retargeting, and your next creative brief.
