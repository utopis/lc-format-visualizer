## Full text

> this is the ACTUAL playbook for scaling an app past $10k/mo with slideshows
> 
> block off 4-6 hours a day for 3 days straight and you walk away with 2+ months of content ready to go
> 
> start with an hour of pure research on tiktok
> 
> -> screenshot every slideshow that's already working in your space
> -> break down the hooks
> -> note what cta they're running
> -> figure out what actually makes someone swipe to slide 2
> 
> after that build a swipe file on pinterest, one board per slide type
> 
> then i run claude code with an image api and generate way more original variations than i could ever make by hand. claude writes the slide text too, so a few good concepts turn into hundreds of posts fast
> 
> socialclaw handles posting it all out across accounts
> 
> 2 posts a day
> 10 accounts
> 20 shots at the algorithm every single day
> 
> most of these WILL flop (even below 300 views)
> 
> but a handful randomly hit 50k, 200k, 500k, every now and then 1M+
> 
> had one blow up to 5M just from this
> 
> the thing nobody talks about is the cta. it's never on the early slides. those are just genuinely good content people want to swipe through on their own
> 
> the app shows up near the end after the viewer is trapped
> 
> once this whole thing is built you're just testing hundreds of hooks and creatives a day without ever manually posting again
> 
> dropped the entire workflow, every hook, every slideshow format i use for this in the free guide
