## Full text

> If you want to focus on organic marketing, do this before you build your next app:
> 
> - Make a TikTok page
> - Follow people in your niche
> - Find the most viral slideshow formats in your niche
> - Copy them with your own flavor
> - Start posting 1 slideshow a day, then work up to 3x a day
> - If you can consistently get 1,000+ views, you have a good format
> - Build the app
> - Add a CTA to your slideshows
> - Make 15 TikTok accounts if you have 2 phones, or 8 if you only have 1
> - Have Claude generate 1,000 slideshows using your proven format
> - Use celebrities or relatable images. Don’t AI-generate the photos
> - Buy @myviralfarm for $39/month
> - Post 2 slideshows a day on 15 accounts with your CTA through ViralFarm
> - Have ViralFarm save them as drafts, then manually add music and post. Takes 5 seconds per post
> - If you’re getting views but no downloads, your slideshows aren’t relevant enough or your App Store page sucks
> - If you’re getting downloads but no conversions, your onboarding or paywall sucks
> - Fix the bottleneck and collect free money
