## Full text

> This is wild 😭
> 
> 4.3M views.
> 250K saves.
> On a "weird habits" slideshow.
> 
> No product demo.
> No feature dump.
> Just aesthetic slides of habits that "actually work".
> 
> And somewhere in the middle:
> the app (Albo) is listed as one of the habits.
> 
> why it works:
> > "weird habits" = curiosity, not a sales pitch
> > every slide feels like a tip from a friend
> > the app is habit #N, not the hero of the post
> > people saved an organization app ad like it was a life hack
> 
> Steal it for your app:
> 1. list 5–7 real tips people would screenshot anyway
> 2. tuck your product in as tip #4 or #5
> 3. make the app look like a habit, not a pitch
> 
> If they'd save the tips without the app, the format works.
> 
> Stop leading with features.
> Lead with habits they'll steal.
