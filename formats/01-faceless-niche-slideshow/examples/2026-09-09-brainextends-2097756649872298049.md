## Full text

> this is how app founders are scaling to $10k/mo with slideshows
> 
> you need to spend like 4-6 hours on this for 3 days  and you are left with 2+ months of content
> 
> first spend an hour researching competitors on tiktok
> 
> -> save their best slideshows
> -> study their hooks
> -> the CTA they use 
> -> what makes you swipe to the next slide
> 
> then go find reference visuals on pinterest and build a library for every slide type
> 
> after that i use claude code + an image API to generate a f*ck ton of original variations from those references
> 
> claude can also write the slide text and turn a few winning concepts into 100s of posts
> 
> then socialclaw schedules it all across multiple tiktok accounts
> 
> 2 posts/day
> 10 accounts
> 20 shots at distribution every single day
> 
> the majority WILL flop
> 
> some won't even hit 300 views
> 
> but a few will randomly do 50k, 200k, 500k, sometimes 1M+
> 
> one of mine hit 5M doing this
> 
> and the important part is the CTA dosent get shoved into peoples faces 
> 
> the early slides are just normal content people actually want to swipe through
> 
> then the app gets introduced naturally near the end
> 
> once the system is built you're basically testing hundreds of hooks and creatives without manually making a new post every day
> 
> i put the full workflow, hooks, slideshow formats + everything i'd use to run this for an app in the free guide:
> 
> https://brainextends-organic-playbook.pages.dev/
