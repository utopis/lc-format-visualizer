## Full text

> this is one of the easiest ways to give every app slideshow a better shot at distribution
> 
> create a tiktok account only for research
> 
> follow nothing except your niche
> 
> like, save and watch the content you want more of
> 
> give it a few days
> 
> then your fyp basically becomes a live feed of what sounds are working
> 
> use those sounds on your posts that same day
> 
> you're attaching your content to something that already has momentum instead of randomly picking audio every time
> 
> and if you're running slideshows properly the effect compounds fast
> 
> research the best posts in your niche
> 
> save the hooks / structure / CTAs
> 
> build a visual library
> 
> use claude + an image API to turn a few good references into a shit ton of original variations
> 
> then schedule them across multiple accounts
> 
> -> 2 posts/day
> -> 10 accounts
> -> 20 attempts at distribution every day
> 
> most of your posts WILL flop 
> 
> some won't even hit 300 views  
> 
> but a few will randomly do 50k, 200k, 500k, sometimes 1M+  
> 
> one of mine hit 5M doing this
> 
> once you pair that with a proper slideshow system, posting gets way easier
