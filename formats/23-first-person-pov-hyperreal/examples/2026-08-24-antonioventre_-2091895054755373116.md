## Full text

> The production rule that decides whether a testimonial-style ad works: the reaction cannot be scripted
> 
> The clearest version is the founder-customer call format. The founder calls a real customer who has no idea the call is coming and just asks: why do you keep ordering, how long have you used it, do you actually see a difference. The spontaneous answers are the whole ad
> 
> The moment you script it, it dies. People spot fake calls and fake reactions instantly, and a slightly messy real reaction beats a perfect scripted one
> 
> Same rule on our POV reaction ads: no face, product filmed from the buyer's point of view, a live unscripted reaction to what it does. Minimal text, minimal editing. The rawness is not a budget limitation, it is what makes the format work
