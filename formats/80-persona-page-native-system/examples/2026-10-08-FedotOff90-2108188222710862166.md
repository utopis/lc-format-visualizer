## Full text

> Prime Prometics has 2,828 active ads.
> 
> Distinct avatars: 24 — all women, ages 40 to 65+. Six are life event avatars: mother of the bride, reunion, divorcee, first date, a local-news clip and the church community.
> Narrator personas: about 28 faces and voices across 11 persona types, run through 15 pages. Creative diversity at its best. 
> Extracted this intel with this 1 prompt from Gethookd MCP:
> 
> You're connected to GetHookd. I'm going to give you one competitor's product page, and I want a complete micro-angle x-ray of how they advertise it — the kind of breakdown that explains how a brand runs hundreds or thousands of active ads on ONE product.
> THE PRODUCT PAGE:  primeprometics . com
> STEP 1 — FIND THEIR LIBRARY. Work out the brand name and the product-category terms from the URL. Search GetHookd for active ads on both, and keep only ads whose landing page contains the brand's domain. Sample at least 50 ads across different launch dates — not 50 clones from one batch. Include subdomains as well in the research.
> STEP 2 — REPORT THE SCALE FIRST. Before any analysis, give me two numbers on their own line: the brand's total active ads right now, and their longest-running ad's age in days.
> STEP 3 — READ THE TRANSCRIPTS, NOT JUST THE COPY. This is critical. Big brands often run near-identical primary text across dozens of ads and put the entire angle inside the video. Pull the transcripts of the video ads. The micro-angle lives in the first five seconds of the voiceover.
> STEP 4 — CLUSTER INTO MICRO-ANGLES. Group every ad by the specific buyer motivation its OPENING targets — not the format. One row per cluster: - Angle name (3-5 words) - The hook, quoted verbatim (first 10-15 words of the VO or copy) - How many ads in the sample run it - Days active of the strongest one + its share link - Awareness level: unaware / problem / solution / product / most-aware - Who it's for: the avatar in a few words (age, gender, situation)
> STEP 5 — THE DIVERSITY AXES. Under the table, four lines: how many awareness levels covered · how many distinct avatars · how many narrator voices or personas · how many creative formats.
> STEP 6 — THE GAPS. 3-5 obvious micro-angles for this product they are NOT running.
> STEP 7 — THE SCALE MATH. Close with two sentences: [number of angles found] distinct micro-angles in this sample alone — each one its own entity with its own variations — and that multiplication is how a brand gets to [total active ads] active ads on one product while most stores stall at ten.
> Output: the two scale numbers, then the cluster table (clean, screenshot-ready), then the axes, the gaps, and the scale math.
