> The parasite angle is a cause-relocation play.
> The viewer walks in blaming their symptom on something ordinary. Diet, age, stress, bad luck. 
> The ad relocates the blame to a hidden culprit living inside them, then tells them only one product can reach it.
> What makes it so powerful is that it absorbs almost any complaint. 
> Bloating, weight, fatigue, sugar cravings, brain fog, thinning hair, bad sleep, joint pain. 
> Every single one gets re-attributed to the same root cause.
