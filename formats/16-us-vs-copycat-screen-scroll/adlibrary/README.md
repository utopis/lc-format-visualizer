## Wave 4: Alex Fedotoff's October 2026 swipe boards (GetHookd public previews)

Each ad below is from the free 10-ad preview of a public GetHookd board that [@FedotOff90](https://x.com/FedotOff90) shared on X. Days live are as of 2026-10-09; brand claims are the advertisers', not verified.

### Penrose Skin: "Wanna pull? You need this fragrance" dupe yapper (Penrose) (89 days live)

![Penrose Skin](w4_119055480.jpg)

- **Board:** [Penrose Skin: 100 Longest-Running 4-5 Star Ads (Oct 2026)](https://x.com/FedotOff90/status/2107841782251671953) · video 28 s
- **What happens:** A man to camera: "Wanna pull fine shit like this, trust me you need to get this fragrance… it literally smells like [designer], the most identical fragrance… a lot more affordable… lasts a lot longer." 28 s.
- **Why it lasts:** 89 days (the brand has 4,358 active ads). A primal hook, a dupe-of-luxury angle and a price anchor.
- **How to make one:** Hook = desire outcome, product = dupe of the expensive thing, 2 advantages (price, longevity).
- **LC remake:** "Want the $2,000 Cartier look? This is the closest I've found, and it's waterproof."

### Penrose Skin: Talking-jar CGI rivalry ("You copied me! That's theft!") (89 days live)

![Penrose Skin](w4_119055278.jpg)

- **Board:** [Penrose Skin: 100 Longest-Running 4-5 Star Ads (Oct 2026)](https://x.com/FedotOff90/status/2107841782251671953) · video 43 s
- **What happens:** A CGI designer-cologne bottle argues with the Penrose jar: "You copied me! That's theft!" / "Can't copyright a scent, babe… And I've got your exact same scent. Plus pheromones. For $220 less." 43 s.
- **Why it lasts:** 89 days. A rivalry skit between product characters dramatizes the dupe claim without a human making it.
- **How to make one:** Two animated product characters (luxury versus yours) argue; yours wins on price and an extra benefit.
- **LC remake:** An animated designer-bracelet versus LC bracelet argument: "Can't copyright gold, babe. And I don't turn green."

### Penrose Skin: "I stopped buying $400 colognes" price-anchor demo (89 days live)

![Penrose Skin](w4_119055288.jpg)

- **Board:** [Penrose Skin: 100 Longest-Running 4-5 Star Ads (Oct 2026)](https://x.com/FedotOff90/status/2107841782251671953) · video 15 s
- **What happens:** Overlay: "I stopped buying $400 colognes… and started smelling like this", a red arrow pointing at the jar, "$ave for ONLY $XX". 15 s.
- **Why it lasts:** 89 days. A clear price-anchor switch story in 15 s.
- **How to make one:** One overlay sentence of the switch, an arrow at the product, a price.
- **LC remake:** "I stopped buying $300 gold chains… and started wearing this."
