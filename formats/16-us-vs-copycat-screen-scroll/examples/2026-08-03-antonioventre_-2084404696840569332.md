## Full text

> Checked the ad library for us-vs-them ads recently. 
> 
> Something like 99% of ecom brands run the comparison ad and then send the click to... their product page. With a little comparison table buried somewhere under the fold.
> 
> If the comparison is your angle, the comparison IS the landing page.
> 
> A dedicated page built entirely around your product next to the market alternative. 
> 
> Real data, side by side, no trash talk, no "their product is bad", just the differences laid out. 
> 
> It can be dressed as a listicle or an advertorial, but its one job is the comparison the ad promised.
> 
> The person clicked to see a comparison. Landing them on a PDP answers a question they did not ask.
