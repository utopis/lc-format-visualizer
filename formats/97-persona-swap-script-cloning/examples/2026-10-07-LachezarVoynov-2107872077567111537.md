## Full text

> A few people asked me what a CREATIVE POD is and how to structure one.
> 
> Here's my short answer:
> 
> A POD is a self-sufficient creative team within your company that can operate on its own.
> 
> Meaning, the whole pod is responsible for the completion of the work process - planning, strategy, and execution.
> 
> Each POD works in isolation from all the other PODs in the company.
> 
> A creative POD for us at my agency consists of:
> 
> 1 Creative Strategist
> 3-4 video editors
> 
> and multiple shared resources (positions) between a few pods:
> 
> - 1 Creative Coordinator: sources the content creators, sends them the briefs, and makes sure the right footage comes back
> on time.
> 
> - 1 Project Manager: keeps every pod on schedule, tracks deadlines, and makes sure nothing falls through the cracks.
> 
> - 1 Graphic Designer: handles statics and design assets for the pods.
> 
> The work of the POD is overseen by the Project Manager, who reports to my Operations Manager.
> 
> I also have a Head of Video Editing who's the person responsible for overseeing the work of each video editor.
> 
> Each POD has KPIs that they need to meet every week + 2 meetings per week: 
> 
> - one to set the agenda for the week
> - one to review what's been completed throughout the week and where problems are being raised.
> 
> The POD should be led by a Lead Creative Strategist - in my case, this is me, but in the very near future I will bring a team member on board to replace me.
> 
> That Lead Creative Strategist should participate in the strategy and will be responsible for the performance of the POD. He/She should assist the POD and help them produce better performance for the brand or agency clients.
>
