## Full text

> How to find new winning ads when your old ones stop working:
> 
> Diagnose first:
> - sort your ads by spend over the last 30 days
> - if your top 5 are 90+ days old, nothing new is winning
> - more ads won't fix that, you need new reasons to buy
> - screenshot your last 30 ads into one grid
> - if they all look the same, Meta treats them like one ad
> - count your angles (the reasons to buy), not your ads
> - 100 versions of one idea is still only one idea tested
> - check if your "different" personas are really one person
> - e.g. 6 personas, all the same age with the same problem
> - check which awareness levels get $0 spend
> - e.g. nothing aimed at people who don't know they have the problem
> - can't say which ads died + why? you haven't tested everything
> 
> Find new angles + formats:
> - read refund reasons + support tickets, not just 5 star reviews
> - complaints + doubts give you angles, 5 star reviews don't
> - listen back to your sales + customer service calls
> - write down the exact words customers use
> - look for buyers you've never made an ad for
> - e.g. gift buyers, partners, a different age group
> - make a fresh TikTok account + scroll it as your customer
> - save every ad format you've never tested
> - save competitor ads that have run for 90+ days
> - write down why people buy, not what the ad looks like
> 
> Stop doing:
> - briefing "make something like our best ad"
> - it makes a weaker copy of an ad Meta already trusts
> - changing the image + headline and calling it a new test
> - shipping 100+ small tweaks a week to feel productive
> - using 25 year old creators to sell to 45+ buyers
> - turning ads off after one bad day of CPA
> - keeping the whole strategy in the founder's head
> 
> Test + scale:
> - test one new reason to buy before 10 new hooks on an old one
> - give new ads enough budget to compete with your old winner
> - test in this order: angles, then hooks, then formats
> - judge on 7 days of blended CAC (total spend ÷ new customers)
> - not on one day of numbers in Ads Manager
> - when a new angle wins, make more versions of it
> - new creator, new format, new problem it solves
> 
> Build it so it runs without you:
> - one spreadsheet that lists every ad you launch
> - tag each one by angle, persona, awareness level + format
> - log when each ad died + how long it lasted
> - your team should be able to explain why each winner won
> - every week, review what got spend + which persona bought
> - then brief new ads for the gaps with $0 spend
> 
> The goal = a new winning ad every month, and a team that knows why it won, without it all living in your head.
