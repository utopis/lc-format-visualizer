> 10 winning AI UGC styles you can test on your brand right now:
> 
> 📁 Talking-Head Testimonial
> ├── Selfie framing, one creator, straight to camera
> ├── Bedroom, kitchen, or bathroom setting
> └── "I've been using this for 2 weeks and..."
> 
> 📁 Product-in-Hand
> ├── Creator holding and presenting the product
> ├── Product visible the entire time
> └── Casual phone-video aesthetic
> 
> 📁 First-Try Reaction
> ├── Creator opens or applies the product on camera
> ├── Sells with the face, not the script
> └── Skepticism turning into surprise
> 
> 📁 Fake Podcast
> ├── Two people, mics, studio setup
> ├── Product comes up mid-conversation
> └── Skeptic gets convinced by the other person
> 
> 📁 Street Interview
> ├── Handheld mic, stranger answers a question
> ├── The question is the hook
> └── Answer reads as opinion, not a pitch
> 
> 📁 Comment Reply
> ├── Real comment or FAQ pinned on screen
> ├── Creator answers it with the product in use
> └── TikTok-reply energy
> 
> 📁 Unboxing
> ├── Package in frame, unwrap, first look
> ├── Texture, smell, packaging details
> └── Short verdict at the end
> 
> 📁 Day-in-the-Life / GRWM
> ├── Product woven into a morning or skincare routine
> ├── Multiple scenes, same creator
> └── Not a hard sell until the last few seconds
> 
> 📁 Before / After
> ├── Same framing, same person
> ├── Product is the bridge between the two
> └── Works for skincare, fitness, home, wellness
> 
> 📁 Whiteboard Explainer
> ├── Creator teaches the mechanism on camera
> ├── Whiteboard, notepad, or paper taped to their back
> └── Looks like a mentor, not an ad
>
