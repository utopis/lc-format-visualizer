## Full text

> for anyone looking to crack cold traffic on demand gen
> 
> many of our brands are spending $10k-$20k/day rn
> 
> we use a strategy framework to iterate and ship new creatives fast asf.
> 
> here’s how it works:
> 
> > swap the hook
> 
> keep the core 30-90 second body exactly the same.
> 
> then write 5+ new hooks around the winner and test different concepts
> 
> > go for new lead structures: keep the big idea the same, but change how you lead people into it.
> 
> - story leads
> - listicle leads
> - hype leads (lean into virality and trending formats)
> - problem & benefit leads
> - misconception leads (“people think x is causing y, but …”)
> - information leads (leads with interesting fact, hack, statistic, etc)
> 
> you can also cut or extend the problem agitation part depending on the product
> 
> > test different video lengths. we create both shorter and longer versions of the same ad (cut filler, shorten intro, introduce the product earlier, etc)
> 
> > change the closing sections (social proof, guarantee, cta, urgency, scarcity, offers, etc).
> 
> > try new visuals:
> 
> - split-screen layouts (e.g., product on one side, normal script other side)
> - different b-rolls
> - different visual styles throughout the ad
> 
> > adapt winning scripts into new formats (ugc, ai animation, podcast, mini vsl, founder story, promo ad, etc)
> 
> > take an image ad angle into a video (and vice versa)
> 
> when it comes to testing, we keep the structure simple.
> 
> for high spend accounts, we use the 1-1-1 structure:
> 
> 1 campaign, 1 ad group, 1 ad
> 
> if the budget is more limited, we'll bundle up to 5 creatives into the same ad group instead.
> 
> as for spend, we'll budget around 0.4x-1x our target prospecting CPA per day for each test.
> 
> once a creative is hitting KPIs, we also start testing new audiences, markets, offers, funnels, placements.
