## Full text

> 3 things I've learned writing and strategizing ads for 8-figure brands: 
> 
> 1 - There's an infinite amount of WINNING ads to write
> 
> Most brands pick 5-6 personas and say that's who they're writing to. 
> 
> But once you lay out: 
> 
> • the different segments you can write to - personas, symptoms, situations they're in, exact desires, etc.
> 
> • awareness levels - unaware, problem aware, solution aware, product, and most aware
> 
> • different ways to write - new hooks, angles, stories, vibes, tones, voices
> 
> • Creative styles - UGC, VSLs, freaking pixar, claymation styles, rankings, tiktok love letter, etc.
> 
> ....then you realize you'll NEVER run out of ideas.
> 
> And you can always find a new persona/segment of people to target.
> 
> 2 - Think like a scroller, not just a copywriter
> 
> There's a reason why some UGC creators are 10x better scripters than even many copywriters.
> 
> Because they know what actually stops the scroll and what people want to hear when they're on their phones.
> 
> Too many writers still write to impress other writers, not actually get the audience's attention. 
> 
> 3 - Copy is just one part of the job
> 
> A lot of copywriters became strategists...(me included)
> 
> Not realizing they're not just writing words on a Gdoc.
> 
> They're also: 
> 
> • briefing designers
> • recommending hook visuals
> • covering the whole vibe and tone of an ad
> 
> It's not easy.
> 
> It's tedious and kinda annoying at times.
> 
> But they're ain't nothing like building a winner that you had direction one every part.
