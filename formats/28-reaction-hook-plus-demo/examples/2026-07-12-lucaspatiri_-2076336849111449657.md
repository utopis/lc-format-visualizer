## Full text

> I analyzed 500+ UGC videos across 30 apps. 9 hooks showed up in 80% of the top performers.
> 
> Here's the exact list (bookmark this):
> 
> 1. The "POV" hook
> "POV: you finally find an app that..."
> Makes the viewer the main character. Top format on TikTok and Reels right now.
> 
> 2. The reaction discovery
> Someone finds the app on camera for the first time. No script. Just genuine surprise.
> This is the highest-converting hook in app UGC.
> 
> 3. The "that friend who..."
> "That friend who thinks everything is aesthetic"
> WayShot used this for 48.6M views. It works because people tag themselves.
> 
> 4. The before/after transformation
> Show the problem, then the result. No talking needed. Cal AI used this a lot
> 
> 5. The guilty confession
> "[Identity] who [relatable guilty thing]"
> Self-categorizing content. People watch because they feel seen.
> 
> 6. The pure question hook
> "What app did you use for that?"
> 
> 7. The surprising stat opener
> Drop a number in the first 0.5 seconds. On-screen text, not voiceover.
> Videos with text in the first half-second had 60%+ hook rates.
> 
> 8. The "I was doing it wrong" story
> "I was tracking everything in a spreadsheet like an idiot"
> Switch stories convert because they validate the viewer's frustration.
> 
> 9. The routine integration
> "Tools in my daily stack"
> No hard sell. The app just lives inside a lifestyle the viewer already wants.
> 
> The pattern across all 9: none of them feel like ads.
> That's the only rule that matters.
