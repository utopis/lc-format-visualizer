## Full text

> A call ad variation that is crushing for us right now: customer service call ads
> 
> Instead of a testimonial praising the product, you record a customer service call. A real pre-purchase conversation: the customer asks the questions people actually ask before buying, and support answers them one by one
> 
> The key is where the questions come from. Pull them from your real support inbox and comment sections, because the people watching the ad are stuck on the exact same 5 or 6 questions
> 
> It works because every unanswered question is a lost purchase. Most ads pile on more claims, this format removes the objections instead. The viewer hears their own doubt asked out loud and answered before they ever reach the product page
