## Full text

> Call ads are quietly becoming their own category on Meta. Every variation of a recorded conversation is working for us right now:
> 
> - FaceTime call ads, two people on a recorded video call talking about the product
> - Founder-customer calls, the founder calls a real customer who doesn't know the call is coming, and we record the reaction
> - Customer service call ads, real pre-purchase questions answered one by one
> - WhatsApp voice message ads, a screen-recorded chat with voice notes going back and forth
> 
> All of them work for the same reason: a conversation doesn't look like an ad, and people trust what they feel they are overhearing more than what is said to them directly.
