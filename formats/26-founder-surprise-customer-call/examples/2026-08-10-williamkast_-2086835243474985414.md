## Full text

> We've been turning 1 winning ad into 5 with this framework.
> 
> Once you have a winning ad, you can package it into different ad formats to appeal to different sub-audiences of your persona.
> 
> I know many do that already.
> 
> But simply running different ad formats is NOT creative diversity.
> 
> There are 2 types of creative diversity.
> 
> 1. Production Format
> 
> 2. Creative Framework
> 
> Most leverage only the production format.
> 
> I.e., Founders ads vs UGC ads vs Static Ads, etc
> 
> But the creative framework adds just as much creative diversity.
> 
> For example, you can pick UGC as the production format, but then use UGC to make a:
> - day in a life ad
> - 3 reasons why ad
> - old me talking to new me ad
> - phone call ad
> - etc
> 
> This gives you a matrix of creative diversity.
> 
> You can combine most creative formats with most production formats.
> 
> The beauty of this is that all of this can be an iteration of just 1 winning message.
> 
> Infinite winning ads.
