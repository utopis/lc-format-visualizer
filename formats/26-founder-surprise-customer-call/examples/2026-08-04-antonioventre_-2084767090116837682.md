## Full text

> An ad format that is going really crazy for us right now: the surprise customer call.
> 
> The founder calls a real customer. The customer doesn't know the call is coming. We record it.
> 
> The founder just asks normal questions. Why do you keep ordering? What changed since you started using it? Would you recommend it?
> 
> The key is that nothing can be scripted. The customer's surprise is the whole ad. People have become very good at spotting staged testimonials, coached answers and AI voices. A real person caught off guard cannot be faked.
> 
> It works because it is a testimonial that doesn't look like one. You are not watching an actor say the product is good, you are listening to a real conversation where someone happens to love the product.
