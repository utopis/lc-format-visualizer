## Full text

> I have been to 3 separate Masterminds to listen to @itshudsonblake, founder of Billion dollar fashion and lifestyle company @comfrt.
> 
> This is the "Hudson method" (as @Seanfrank and many others dub it) that he used and still uses to this day to massively grow his business from being homeless at 16 to being on track to hit $1 billion in revenue in just 3 years.
> 
> Send product to hundreds or even thousands of small, new creators on TikTok per month.
> 
> Pay creators per video PLUS commission with a HEAVY focus on incentivizing them to post as many videos per sample as possible. The more videos a creator posts, the bigger the bonus you should give them. Hudson gives MASSIVE bonuses for creators that post 100 or more videos a month something in the $5,000+ range if I remember correctly.
> 
> Take all the creatives you're getting from these creators and load it into every ad channel, Meta, Snapchat, Youtube, etc to create an infinite number of new ad angles with constant testing to identify and double down on winning concepts and creators.
> 
> Scale your ad spend on all platforms, constantly tweaking, adding videos, picking winning creatives, all while still paying the creators commission on the ad sales to keep them incentivized to make MORE content.
> 
> This strategy is all about the "free" cpms you get from TikTok Shop creators + the infinite number of content and angles you can test across all the channels and THAT is what boosts your business 10x, 100x even 1000x.
> 
> More ads, more angles, more channels, more eyeballs, more iterations = More SALES and more GROWTH for your business.
