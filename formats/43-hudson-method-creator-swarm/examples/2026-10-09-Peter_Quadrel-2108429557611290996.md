## Full text

> Creator ads keep spending long after your other ads fade
> 
> A year of Meta data shows creator ads lasting 1.8x longer than brand video
> 
> I tracked every ad across our accounts for a full year, from its first dollar to its last
> 
> To measure how long ads last I used restricted mean survival time, a metric from clinical trials
> 
> It counts how many of an ad's first 90 days it stays live and spending
> 
> - Partnership (creator) ads: 50.5 days
> - Static: 38.4 days
> - Brand-run video: 28.1 days
> 
> At day 90, 29% of creator ads were still spending, against about 11% of statics
> 
> The spend curves explain why
> 
> - Brand video spends 92% of its lifetime budget in the first two weeks
> 
> - Static spends 59% in the first two weeks
> 
> - Creator ads spend 36%, then hold $20 to $28 a day for two months
> 
> How to use this
> 
> - Keep creator ads that are still spending in month two out of your normal rotation
> 
> - Give new creator ads more time before cutting, since their spend builds slower
> 
> - Run one partner per ad set so you can see which creators carry spend
> 
> - Start partnership ads at 10% of Meta budget and work toward 20%
> 
> Most creative is built to be replaced. Creator ads keep paying rent, so build your production around them
