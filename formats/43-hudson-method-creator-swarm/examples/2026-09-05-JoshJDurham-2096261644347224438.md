> Just broke down one of my most in-depth guides on which influencer marketing lever to pull based on your revenue and AOV.
> 
> > Seeding - free product, no obligation to post
> > Gifting - free product in exchange for agreed deliverables
> > Whitelisting - paid access to run ads through a creator's handle
> > Partnership ads - Meta's tool for approving brand ads on a creator's page
> > The Hudson Method - retainer plus a percentage of ad spend for daily volume
> > Trybe - the tool that automates the Hudson Method
> > TikTok Shops - gifting to a thousand creators a month for affiliate reach
