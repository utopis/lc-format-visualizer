> It just keeps going! Seemingly the most simple talking head video too. But this is why I think this ad is working:
> 
> 1. Filming in the car makes it look more organic. Couldn’t even wait to go inside to tell you!!
> 2. I told a story in it the whole time.
> 3. I started with an authority pull “my aunt’s a gyno and she told me xyz”
> 4. Honestly, I’m great at face to camera yapping. With a lot of practice it really starts to feel like you’re just telling your friend on FaceTime about a product you like. 
> 5. This particular ad doesn’t have much flashy editing. For this organic storytime yapper style, flashy editing takes away from the story imo. 
> 6. I showed the product and moved it in my hands when I was talking, rather than just sitting and staring at the camera.
