## Full text

> We currently oversee $25M in monthly Meta spend
> 
> Most of our clients are making $1m+/mo just from Meta
> 
> Here’s what scales:
> 
> 1. Top of Funnel creative
> 
> This is extremely hard to crack but I’ve shared many creative examples of TOF winners that actually scale ad accounts.
> 
> The biggest unlocks we see are coming from:
> 
> - direct-response scripts that tell a story
> - Suno songs (that tell a story)
> - Authority figures explaining a problem (attaching an example below)
> 
> What A LOT of brand owners don’t understand is that Top of Funnel creatives need a higher CPA tolerance.
> 
> You can’t expect them to scale at the same CPA as your MOF/BOF ad creatives that push your limited-time offer.
> 
> We ran into this problem with an 8-figure brand that everyone is spying on.
> 
> One of our ad creatives actually went viral and started getting copied by other 8- and 9-figure DTC brands.
> 
> The truth is that the creative was not scaled because it was not able to sustain the CPA of their BOF ads.
> 
> The path to 9-figures comes from tracking the % of New Visitors each ad creative brings and having an allocated amount you can afford to attract New Customers.
> 
> If you’re simply looking at ROAS or blended CPA, you won’t scale past $10M/month.
> 
> 2. Internal creative team + 2-3 top agencies
> 
> This is the setup you should build for pumping out winning ads.
> 
> Agencies have such a bad rep, but there are a few agencies on LinkedIn and Twitter that are absolutely cracked when it comes to creating winners.
> 
> Agencies have the benefit of working with high-spending accounts and being able to see what works across the whole DTC landscape.
> 
> Internal teams move much faster.
> 
> That’s why you should have the agency focus on net-new & big swing ideas with a lower likelihood of success, and get your internal team to focus on what already works inside the account but do it a lighting speed.
> 
> There is no point in comparing the creative output of an agency vs the creative output of an in-house team.
> 
> They both serve different purposes.
> 
> 3. Subscriptions
> 
> One of our clients started his eComm brand 2 years ago.
> 
> 6 months ago he hit $1m/month.
> 
> 3 months ago he hit $2m/month.
> 
> He’s currently doing $5M/month in sales.
> 
> All because of subscriptions.
> 
> Scaling a one-time purchase DTC brand to 9-figures is nearly impossible.
> 
> If I ever start my own brand, it’s 100% going to be a subscription-based business.
> 
> High LTV will allow you to outspend anyone in the market and grow faster than anybody else.
> 
> Even if you look at one of the fastest-growing DTC brands ever - IM8 - they scaled past $100M ARR in 11 months simply due to their subscription offer.
> 
> eComm is hard.
> 
> eComm without subscriptions is even harder.
