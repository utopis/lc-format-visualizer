## Full text

> I've been ranting a lot about native ads lately. I love 'em.
> 
> But while they do tickle the algo (and my fancy) in just the right way, "native" is just a way of presentation.
> 
> It's the paint. Not the message.
> 
> And the message is king.
> 
> Take these 3 ads.
> 
> First one (long form letter) surfaces a specific pain by saying what a very specific person is - presumably - thinking.
> 
> Second one (handwritten) is an edge-lord-sexual-innuendo ad that stops the scroll and, hopefully, earns a snicker (and a click). No persona at all.
> 
> Third one (blue) is a direct call-out of a very specific avatar, appealing to their ego.
> 
> Guess which one printed the most?
