## Full text

> my experience in health (specifically the joint pain niche) taught me one thing:
> 
> selling to older audiences will humble your creative strategy real quick.
> 
> you can spend hours building:
> 
> a fucking 2,000-word advertorial.
> a perfectly structured listicle.
> a sophisticated mechanism.
> a beautiful “native” landing page.
> 
> and then some ugly static with a huge headline and a product image absolutely destroys it…
> 
> why?
> 
> older audiences don’t need you to be clever.
> 
> they need you to be CLEAR ASF.
> 
> what hurts?
> why might it be happening?
> what does this do?
> why should i believe you?
> what do i do next?
> 
> that’s it.
> 
> i’ve learned to obsess way less over making an ad feel sophisticated and way more over making it impossible to misunderstand.
> 
> big readable copy. obvious product.
> familiar language. simple demonstrations.
> one argument at a time.
> 
> and sometimes, yes:
> the ugly ad wins.
> the basic product page wins.
> 
> the straightforward explanation beats the 17-step advertorial funnel you spent a week building.
> 
> especially with older demos, friction kills.
> 
> every extra concept they have to understand is another chance to lose them.
