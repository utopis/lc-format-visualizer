## Full text

> ranking every AI UGC format in tiers...
> 
> S TIER:
> 
> - podcast ad. best format for anything that needs explaining.
> 
> - talking head. the cleanest test of whether your angle works.
> 
> - in-car. reads as private and unscripted and is the CHEAPEST to render well.
> 
> - claymation. highest usable rate of any animation style.
> 
> A TIER
> 
> - zach d films. second person escalation. best for problems that compound.
> 
> - street interview. the question does the hooking. converts on social proof.
> 
> - pixar style. warm, safe, and it stages what a camera cannot film.
> 
> - multi scene demo. shows the product working and closes bottom of funnel.
> 
> B TIER
> 
> - reply-to-comment overlay. works on any format but is more of a modifier more than a format.
> 
> - chalkboard explainer. teaches before it sells and good on cold traffic.
> 
> - skeleton story. stops the scroll hard but burns out fastest.
> 
> - split screen day. 2 versions of the same person. it suits energy and sleep.
> 
> C TIER
> 
> - brick animation. fun, narrow use case, and the ip risk is real if you lean on branded characters.
> 
> - static carousel. fine as support but not carrying an account.
> 
> - pure voiceover over b-roll. no face, trust or room to say anything.
