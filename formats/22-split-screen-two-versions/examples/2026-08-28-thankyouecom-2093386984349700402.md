## Full text

> Almost every winner in our scaling CBO this month is AI debate 
> 
> Here’s our method for generating these bangers 
> 
> 1. Not an interview 
> 
> Debate formats are arguments and it feels more like a skit than an ad. 
> 
> 2. Extreme character contrast 
> 
> Make it a younger girl vs an old man or a karen vs a gen z skater. 
> 
> 3. Outside environment 
> 
> Put the characters in a park or street. Don’t go the “podcast” route here. 
> 
> 4. Make it controversial 
> 
> Add a layer of ragebait so you keep the audiences attention. make it crazy. 
> 
> 5. Split screen 
> 
> We’ve had more success with split screen than the camera going back and forth. 
> 
> 6. Test a format transition 
> 
> Put the same debate character in a podcast and have her talk about the debate. 
> 
> This debate format came from an agency we work with. 
> 
> It didn’t even work very well for them haha but I liked it so we copied it. 
> 
> And holy shit it’s been crushing for us ever since
