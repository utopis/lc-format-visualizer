## Full text

> "what is the best ad format?"
> 
> i get this question alllllll the time.
> 
> static vs video, ugc vs founder-led, long form vs six seconds.
> 
> tbh there's no clear winner.
> 
> not because one isn't better than the other, but because the main thing being tested has nothing to do with the format…
> 
> it’s desire.
> 
> what desire are you trying to sell?
> 
> → relief from complexity?
> → fear of falling behind?
> → wanting to look like someone who has their life together?
> 
> pick that first, and the format stops mattering as much.
> 
> a founder explaining why they built one solution instead of seven, a split screen of the old routine against the new one, someone filming themselves throwing out the six bottles they used to need
> 
> all three sell the exact same desire wearing a different outfit.
> 
> test the desire before you test the wrapper, or the winner you find won't tell you anything you can reuse next time.
