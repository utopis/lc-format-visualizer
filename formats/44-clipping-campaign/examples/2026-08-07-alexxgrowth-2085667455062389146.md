> doordash did $13.7 BILLION in revenue last year
> 
> they have one of the best marketing teams on the planet and they just launched something called CringeMart, a store for all the stuff people are embarrassed to buy in real life
> 
> then they put live billboards up in new york and la with a real time counter showing how often someone orders condoms on the app
> 
> a condom sells on doordash every 37 seconds btw
> 
> it's a genuinely brilliant stunt because they took a number off an internal dashboard and made it a crazy landmark
> 
> but here's what they did that all of you missed
> 
> a billboard ONLY reaches the people who walk past it
> 
> so doordash launched a $20,000 campaign on Content Rewards paying creators to clip that footage and put it in front of everybody else
> 
> the billboard individually only reaches thousands 
> 
> but the clips reach millions
> 
> this is what brands still get wrong, they spend the entire budget making something and nothing getting it seen
> 
> a company doing $13.7 billion in revenue doesn't run a $20k clipping campaign because it's cheap
> 
> they run it because they've already done the math on what a billboard costs per impression versus what a clip costs per view
> 
> if doordash is doing this, you probably should be too
> 
> no need to thank me for the sauce (:
