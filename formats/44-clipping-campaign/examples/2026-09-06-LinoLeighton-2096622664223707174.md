> I ran a clipping campaign at 10X ROAS previously.
> 
> Here’s exactly how I did it:
> 
> • Paid anywhere from $0.20–$0.60 CPM depending on the country
> • Sourced clippers directly from TikTok
> • Had each clipper post 2–3 TikTok slides using the format below 
> • Put my app logo + name inside the CTA slide of EVERY creative( in the article)
> 
> That last part is huge.
> 
> You’re building brand recognition while getting organic distribution.
> 
> Then when those same people see your app again through paid ads, they subconsciously recognize the logo/name → making them more likely to convert.
> 
> A few more things:
> 
> • Make every clipper tag your app account in the bio of each post
> • Comment on every post from alt accounts acting like users: “yo this app is so good”
> 
> The goal isn’t just views.
> 
> For managing the clippers, you can do it yourself through Discord or hire an agency manager.
> 
> I HIGHLY recommend the agency route.
> 
> Most take a rev share, but IMO it’s 100% worth it.
> 
> You can focus on the actual business while they handle recruiting, managing, tracking, and scaling the clipping operation.
>
