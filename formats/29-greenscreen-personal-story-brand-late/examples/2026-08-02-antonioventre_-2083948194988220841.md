## Full text

> Green screen reaction ads are still one of our most reliable formats, and most brands build them wrong.
> 
> The setup: a base video plays (a story, a demo, an AI explanation), and a creator sits on top of it reacting. Pausing it, commenting, asking questions.
> 
> The key is the reactor's job. They are not there to hype the product, they are there to say what the viewer is thinking. "No way this works." "Okay wait, that actually makes sense."
> 
> It works because the reaction layer does two things at once: it holds attention, two videos moving instead of one, and it handles objections in real time through a person who sounds like the viewer, not like the brand.
