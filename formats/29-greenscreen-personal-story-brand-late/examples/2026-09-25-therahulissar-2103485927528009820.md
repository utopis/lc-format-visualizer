## Full text

> We're seeing a tonne of trends on different visual formats outside of just AI.
> 
> To name a few:
> 
> 1/ Greenscreen with no product. We've seen running purely just greenscreen talking about problem for a very long time period and then talking about the specific brand as the solution crushes. 
> 
> Your creator essentially is just yapping about a personal story that they've faced in their life and goes very in depth on it to hold the viewers attention and get bought into the video. 
> 
> The viewer then gets sold on the brand later on in the video because the video feels so organic that they don't expect it to be an ad and when it comes time to convert they have high intent.
> 
> 2/ Natives - These have been working for a while but taking your best performing script and slapping it on a native ad works well. We've seen driving this to a quiz funnel works better than just PDP/listicle. Reason being is these creatives usually have a CTR and but low action rate when the customer gets on the website. 
> 
> So taking them through a quiz funnel helps build purchase intent by the time they get towards the end. 
> 
> 3/ Founder calling customers.
> 
> These work not just as one hit wonders but continuously can deliver bangers for brands.
> 
> Getting the founder on more ads outside of just dialling top customers lets you see more behind the scenes of a brand.
