## Full text

> Native ads, end to end, for anyone who wants to build one
> 
> The image
> - A normal looking photo, like something a friend posted
> - A bit weird on purpose, so people stop
> - The problem in the picture, not the product
> 
> The first line of the primary text
> - This is the real headline, not the headline field under the image
> - Its only job is the 'See more' click
> 
> The copy
> - First person story, from a situation the reader is in before they know they have a problem
> - Product comes in around two thirds of the way down
> - Anywhere from 2,000 to 5,000+ words, more in a competitive market
> 
> Where it runs from
> - A personal page for a personal story, a theme page for an article style ad
> - Never the brand page
> 
> The page behind it
> - If they read the whole story, a product page can work
> - If they click early, an advertorial or a listicle does the education the copy didn't get to
> 
> One of these ads: $48.6K spent, $186K back, 3.83x
