## Full text

> Three ways we run native ads, all built on a long story in the primary text.
> 
> 1. Personal story. A real selfie and a first-person story. It feels like a friend posting, not a brand selling.
> 
> 2. Transformation story. A before and after photo, then the whole journey in the text. Everything they tried, everything that failed, the low point before the change. The emotion is what keeps people reading.
> 
> 3. Doctor style. A nurse or doctor figure talking about a specific case from their career. The ad shows the professional or something medical, then drives to an advertorial where the doctor explains it. A short VSL inside that advertorial works even better.
> 
> All three lead with emotion and a long story, never with the product. The product shows up near the end, once people are already hooked.
