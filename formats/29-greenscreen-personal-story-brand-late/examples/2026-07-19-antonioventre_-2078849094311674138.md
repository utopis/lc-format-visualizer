## Full text

> Green screen reaction ads are working really well right now. Here is the setup.
> 
> You have a main video, usually a creator or an AI creator telling a story or talking about a problem. Then you put a second person on a green screen on top of it, reacting to that video.
> 
> The reactor comments on what is being said, or just confirms it. "This worked for me too."
> 
> Why it hits: the viewer gets a double confirmation. One person makes the claim, a second person backs it up in real time.
> 
> It feels less like an ad and more like watching someone vouch for it.
> 
> You can do the reactor with a real creator or with AI. Either way, the power is two voices saying the same thing, so the product feels proven instead of pitched.
