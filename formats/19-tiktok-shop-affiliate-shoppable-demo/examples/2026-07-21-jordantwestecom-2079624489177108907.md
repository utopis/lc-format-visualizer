## Full text

> Our agency structure for TikTok Shop is (hopefully helpful)  
> 
> Strategist (and associate strat)  
> 
> Owns the overall plan, weekly priorities, product focus, offer, creator strategy, and client comms  
> 
> Creator / affiliate manager 
> 
> Owns creator outreach, sampling, follow-up, affiliate activation, creator relationships, and getting enough content into market  
> 
> Content lead 
> 
> Owns briefs, hooks, product angles, content quality, creator education, and pulling learnings from what is working.  
> 
> Paid media / GMV Max owner  
> 
> Owns spend, GMV Max, Spark Ads where relevant, creative testing, scaling winning content, and making sure ads are amplifying real signal and also getting usage rights across other platforms.   
> 
> Shop ops owner  
> Owns listings, coupons, samples, inventory flags, product setup, violations, reviews, fulfillment issues, and Seller Center hygiene.  
> 
> Our pods generally serve 5ish brands just so you get scale.   For a smaller brand, one strong person can own strategy and coordination at the start, but they should not be doing everything forever.  
> 
> The biggest mistake we see is splitting “ads” and “content” too cleanly.  
> 
> On TikTok Shop, ads are downstream of content and creator signal. (basically like all platform TBH)  
> 
> So the paid person needs to know what creators are making, what hooks are converting, what products are moving, and what the shop can actually support.
