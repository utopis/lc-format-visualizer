## Full text

> 🔥 This founder ad shows the founder for six seconds.
> 
> A mouth tape brand has run it for two hundred and fifty-one days.
> 
> He opens with: "Mouth taping sounds dangerous until you actually understand what it does to your sleep."
> 
> He says the category's scariest objection before the viewer can think it.
> 
> Because he raises the doubt himself, he isn't the one being doubted, and every claim after that sounds like an answer instead of a pitch.
> 
> Then he's off screen and a voiceover carries the rest.
> 
> founder objection → answer → anatomy diagrams with study credits → other people taped and asleep → jawline bonus → comparison table → link
> 
> The founder's part is short and it's only there for trust. The diagrams and clips after him do the proving, so you need very little founder footage. Stock and customer clips can fill the rest.
> 
> Fix for this week: write down the one thing people fear about your category. Have your founder say it word for word in the first line, then answer it.
> 
> I think we'll see a lot more of these, because a founder who freezes on camera only has to get through one line. 🫡
