## Full text

> Why before and after photos are the best image for a native ad.
> 
> People stop because they see a real change, not a product. A change makes you curious. A product shot makes you scroll.
> 
> Then the long primary text tells the full story behind that change. What the person tried, what failed, what finally worked. The product comes in near the end, after the story has already done the emotional work.
> 
> Then you send the click to an advertorial built on the same angle, which carries the story forward before anyone is asked to buy.
> 
> Lead with the product and you are just another ad in the feed. Lead with the transformation and the story, and people read all the way to the end before they even realise they are being sold to.
