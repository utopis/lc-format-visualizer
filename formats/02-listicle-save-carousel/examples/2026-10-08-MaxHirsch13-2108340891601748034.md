> Prompt your agent: "Make me a 6-slide TikTok photo carousel for [app]: slide 1 is a wide, candid, realistic photo with the hook "5 ways to get [result in your niche]" in white text, and slides 2-6 are genuinely useful numbered tips with specific numbers. Exactly ONE middle slide (never the first or last) mentions the "[app]" app in quotes, inside its tip, with a photo of a phone in a hand showing the real app screen; every other slide is pure value. Generate the images with fal ai: Flux Ultra for any shot with faces, nano-banana/edit with that photo as a reference to keep the same people across slides, and Flux Schnell for cheap b-roll, all with "candid iPhone photo, no text, no logos" in the prompt. Keep the text between 300px from the top and the bottom 25% of a 1080x1920 frame so TikTok's UI doesn't cover it, then write a casual lowercase caption with 4-6 hashtags."
