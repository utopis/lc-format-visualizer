## Full text

> Regarding turning off ads:
> 
> Stop doing this constantly every single day.
> 
> Sometimes an ad with a higher CPA is actually doing an important job: bringing fresh people into the funnel.
> 
> You turn it off, ROAS skyrockets the next day, and you think you've made a great decision.
> 
> Then 2-3 days later, CPAs start climbing again.
> 
> That's the waterfall effect.
> 
> Whenever you turn an ad off, write it down. Track the date. Then watch what happens to the OVERALL campaign performance over the following days.
> 
> Don't judge decisions based on what happened the next day.
> 
> Meta has lag.
> 
> Sometimes the "bad" ad you killed was helping the rest of the account perform.
