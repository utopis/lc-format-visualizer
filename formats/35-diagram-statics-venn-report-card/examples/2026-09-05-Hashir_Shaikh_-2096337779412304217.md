## Full text

> We make 1,000+ statics every month.
> 
> Around 10% are Us vs Them.
> 
> Because showing the difference can be more powerful than simply talking about your product.
> 
> Show people what they’re choosing between.
> 
> Brands use static ads to say why their product is good. 
> But “better quality,” “better results,” and “better value” don’t mean much without context.
> 
> An Us vs Them ad gives people that context.
> 
> You can compare your product against the old way of doing something, a common alternative, a generic option, or even the problem itself.
> 
> The key is to compare one thing that actually matters.
> 
> Price.
> Ingredients.
> Time.
> Results.
> Quality.
> Convenience.
> 
> Then make that difference obvious in the creative.
> 
> Because when people can quickly see what’s different and why it matters, your product becomes much easier to understand.
