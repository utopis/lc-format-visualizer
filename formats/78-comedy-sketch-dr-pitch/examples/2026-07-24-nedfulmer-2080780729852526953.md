## Full text

> “Is influencer marketing dead?"
> 
> While hiking through a medieval castle (lol, I know), I recently had a conversation with a founder who had shifted nearly all of his spend to UGC. Why?
> 
> He could set a budget, outline creative (hooks, script, CTA) and it got parceled out to a bunch of random people who would talk about his brand from (usually) their cars, then he *only paid based on performance*
> 
> Previously, his micro-influencers had been overcharging him for content that often flopped in an algorithmic environment they couldn’t control.
> 
> But, when he actually showed me one of the best performing UGC clips?
> 
> It was a comedy sketch with no real CTA, no brand lift. You couldn’t learn anything about his app unless you clicked below the fold in the description.
> 
> Hmm. An effective investment? Idk. Seems more like he was funding someone else’s sketch comedy career.
> 
> So what’s the solution?
> 
> When I asked him if he had ever tried making founder-led content he said no he didn’t have the time or drive or skills.
> 
> Yet he had just eloquently explained his whole business to me in a way that was quite compelling. We could have filmed the whole thing.
> 
> As a founder, there is no one who knows your business better than you.
> 
> Be your own influencer. Make your own content.
> 
> Ride your own algorithmic sails into the sunset and gain the earned reach for free when something inevitably pops off.
> 
> Or just go for a walk and touch grass every once in a while.
