## Full text

> the 5 ad creative formats that convert highest in dropshipping right now ranked by performance:
> 
> 1. UGC-style problem/solution - real person (or AI-generated person) describing the problem they had, showing the product, showing the result. 30-45 seconds. phone-recorded feel not studio. "I was waking up every night with back pain. my friend told me about this. first night I slept through." converts highest because it looks like a friend recommending something not an ad. the less polished it looks the more it converts
> 
> 2. "TikTok made me buy it" format - fast cuts. trending audio. product demo in under 15 seconds. "the algorithm knew I needed this." works for impulse products under $40. the format itself signals "other people are buying this" which is social proof built into the structure
> 
> 3. us vs them comparison - split screen. your product on one side. "the other brand" on the other. exaggerated comparison. your product works perfectly. the other falls apart. the visual comparison replaces 1,000 words of copy. 15-20 seconds. highest CTR of any format because the visual conflict stops the scroll
> 
> 4. founder/operator talking head - you (or a spokesperson) talking directly to camera. "I designed this because I was tired of [problem]." builds brand trust. converts lower on first view but retargets at 2-3x because the face creates familiarity. the face IS the retargeting. people remember faces not products
> 
> 5. slideshow with text overlays - 5-7 product images with benefit text on each. "problem → solution → proof → offer." works on meta stories and reels. lowest production cost of all formats. no filming needed. takes 15 minutes to make. converts at 70-80% of video performance for 5% of the effort
> 
> what all 5 have in common: they feel native to the platform. they don't look like ads. the moment a creative looks "produced" the brain registers "advertisement" and scrolls. every format above is designed to look like content not marketing. the best ad is the one that doesn't look like an ad
