> There are 4 decisions that separate a dropshipping store from a real ecom brand.
> 
> The pattern in almost every store that gets stuck is the same. Revenue comes in. Nothing builds on itself.
> 
> The fix is never the product and rarely the ad. It is almost always one of these four things.
> 
> 1) Stop chasing products and start building a customer. Define exactly who buys from you and make every decision through that lens instead of through what is trending that week.
> 
> 2) Fix what the ads are sending traffic to before you scale the spend. Photography, page copy, offer structure, everything. The conversion happens on the page, not in the ad.
> 
> 3) Build retention before you grow acquisition. A customer you keep costs a fraction of a new one you have to find. Repeat purchase rate should be watched as closely as ROAS.
> 
> 4) Systemize before you hire. Every process documented before a single person is brought in to run it. Hiring without a process is just paying someone to create chaos at scale.
