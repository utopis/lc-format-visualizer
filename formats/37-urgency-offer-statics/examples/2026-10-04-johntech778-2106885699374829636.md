> "Buy 2 Get 1 Free" is a stronger offer than "33% off."
> Same discount. Better conversion.
>  
> Here's why and how I structured a Pawfect product page around it 👇
>  
> 9 conversion decisions most pet DTC brands get wrong:
> 
> 1. Countdown timer in the top bar "PAWSOME SUMMER SALE · 01:59:45 · Save Up To 50% + Free Shipping." Urgency with a reason (seasonal sale). Buyers need a why, not just a when.
> 
> 2. "100% Satisfaction Guarantee" badge on the hero top-right corner of the product image. Risk reversal before the headline. For pet parents who've bought products their dog rejected, this is huge.
> 
> 3. Product name + emotional outcome "Wholesome Treats for Tail-Wagging Happiness." Not "Peanut Butter & Banana Bites." Emotional benefit first. SKU second.
> 
> 4. Benefit description focused on the buyer's relationship with the dog "Perfect for training, rewarding, or just showing your pup some love." This is relationship-driven copy, not feature copy.
> 
> 5. 3 checkmarked feature icons Real Ingredients, Great for Training, Easy to Digest. Simple. Clean. Buyer self-qualifies in 2 seconds.
> 
> 6. "Limited Time Offer – Ends Soon!" with a clock icon reinforces the top countdown. Second urgency signal. This is where pet DTC and supplement DTC share DNA.
> 
> 7. BOGO bundle stack the star of this page:
> • Single Pack: $14.99 (90 treats), crossed from $19.99
> • Buy 2 Get 1 Free: $29.98 (180 treats), "Most Popular", crossed from $59.97 50% off
> • Buy 3 Get 2 Free: $49.98 (450 treats), "Best Value", crossed from $99.95 50% off
> 
> 8. Trust stack below the bundles  30-Day Money Back Guarantee, Fast & Free Shipping on Orders Over $35, 100% Secure Checkout. Three signals at the point of decision.
> 
> 9. Accordion tabs + dog photography throughout  "What's Inside?", "Feeding Guide", "Our Guarantee." Plus 4 supporting images: dog with package, "Made with goodness" ingredients, "Delicious taste dogs love," "Perfect for training or treating." This is UGC-adjacent emotional proof. 
> The pattern: urgency with a reason, emotional copy, BOGO bundle stack, and risk reversal at every level.
