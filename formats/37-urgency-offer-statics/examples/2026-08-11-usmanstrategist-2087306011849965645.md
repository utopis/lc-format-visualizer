> Here's a breakdown of the three funnel stages and the best creative formats for each, so you can drive more purchases and scale your brand more profitably.
> 
> 🔵 TOP OF FUNNEL (TOFU) - Cold Audience
> 
> They're either unaware (they don't know they have a problem or that you exist) or problem-aware (they know the problem but haven't discovered your solution yet).
> 
> Your only job here is to earn three seconds of attention, make the problem feel real, and build trust - not sell.
> 
> Educate them about the problem, introduce your product, and position it as the unique solution.
> 
> Best creative formats for TOFU:
> 
> → UGC-style content
> → Brand story videos
> → Founder-led ads
> → Podcast-style ads
> → Meme-style or relatable content
> → Educational "how it works" explainer videos
> 
> Objective: Make people aware of their problem and introduce your product as the solution. There's no need for hard selling yet.
> 
> 🟡 MIDDLE OF FUNNEL (MOFU) - Warm Audience
> 
> They are solution-aware and product-aware. They've seen your ad, visited your website, or engaged with your content. They know solutions exist, but they haven't decided that your product is the right one.
> 
> Show comparisons and explain why they should choose your product over the hundreds of alternatives on the market.
> 
> Best creative formats:
> 
> → Carousel ads
> → Product unboxing/demo videos
> → Comparison guides or "vs." content
> → Testimonial and social proof ads
> → Influencer or creator collaboration content
> → Behind-the-scenes or "how it's made" content
> → FAQ or objection-handling ads (answer their questions before they type them into Google)
> 
> Objective: Prove that you're the best option on the market and build enough trust and consideration to earn the purchase.
> 
> 🔴 BOTTOM OF FUNNEL (BOFU) – Hot Audience
> 
> They are the most-aware audience. These are site visitors, cart abandoners, and people who've seen everything but haven't completed their purchase.
> 
> Your job is to remove friction and give them a compelling reason to buy today instead of "later" (which usually means never).
> 
> Best creative formats:
> 
> → Dynamic Product Ads (automatically showing the exact product they viewed)
> → Urgency-led single-image ads
> → Customer video testimonials with text overlays
> → Before-and-after ads
> → Limited-time discount or countdown offers
> → Trust-building creatives (money-back guarantees, free shipping, star ratings)
> → Bundle or upsell offers for people already showing purchase intent
> 
> Objective: Drive conversions and generate sales. This is where you'll often see the highest conversion rates.
> 
> So, what's the takeaway?
> 
> Don't run the same creative at every stage of the funnel. Match the creative format to where your customer actually is in their buying journey, and you'll dramatically improve your results.
