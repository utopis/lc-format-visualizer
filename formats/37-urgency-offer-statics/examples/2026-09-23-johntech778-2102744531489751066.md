> 10 conversion decisions most supplement brands get wrong:
> 
> 1. Trust bar at the very top Free Shipping on orders $50+, Trusted by 18,000+ Happy Customers, 30-Day Money Back Guarantee. Three signals before a single scroll.
> 
> 2. Countdown timer on the offer "LIMITED TIME OFFER: UP TO 25% OFF + FREE SHIPPING ON ORDERS $75+" with a live 02:18:37 timer. Urgency isn't a gimmick it's a decision accelerant.
> 
> 3. "6,200+ Bottles Sold This Month" next to the reviews this is demand proof, not just social proof. It tells the buyer "people are buying this right now."
> 4. Headline + benefit subhead "Ashwagandha Gummies" + "Relax. Restore. Unwind. Naturally." Category + outcome in 5 words.
> 5. 4 benefit icons Reduces Stress & Anxiety, Helps Improve Sleep, Supports Mood & Focus, Plant-Based & Non-GMO. Buyer self-qualifies in 2 seconds.
> 
> 6. Subscribe & Save vs One-time toggle Subscribe is the DEFAULT. 20% off. This single move lifts supplement LTV 30–50%.
> 
> 7. 3-tier bundle stack with real math 1 Bottle (15% off, $24.99) → 2 Bottles (20% off, $45.99, "BEST VALUE") → 3 Bottles (25% off, $64.99). The middle anchors. The top captures AOV. The bottom is a decoy.
> 
> 8. Flavor + convenience microcopy "Delicious Mixed Berry Flavor. Easy to chew. No gelatin. Just calm." This is objection-killing copy disguised as product description.
> 
> 9. "In Stock – Ships Within 24 Hours" immediacy + availability. Western buyers trust brands that ship fast.
> 
> 10. Trust stack + payment icons at the CTA 30-Day Guarantee, Secure Checkout, Easy Returns + Visa, Mastercard, Amex, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay. Every friction point removed.
> 
> The pattern: urgency, demand proof, self-qualification, subscription by default, bundle anchors, and zero friction at checkout.
