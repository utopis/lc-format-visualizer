## Full text

> As you start to scale on meta you'll hit a cap on the audience you can talk to.
> 
> The best way to expand out of that is couple of ways:
> 
> 1. Format Change - use the same messaging and change the way it's displayed. AI video, statics, high res vs low rest 
> 
> 2. Start looking at adding new audiences you can talk to. 
> 
> Number 2 is the best way to actually double or triple your spend. You want to figure out how to talk to a completely unaware audience, those creatives are going to fill your audience and also provide you with the ability to spend significantly more at the same or slightly higher CAC.
> 
> With the ideal account setup you'll notice a winning ad will get spend within 72 hours of going live. That's the duration of time required to find a true ad that will scale in the account.
> 
> Until then you have to keep iterating and adjusting your messaging to make sure you speak to the right audience.
